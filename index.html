About Vespera

Connecting people with events, cultural experiences, technology conferences, music festivals and workshops throughout Nepal.

Vespera is a modern event discovery platform built for Nepal. We partner with organizers, innovators, and cultural custodians across Kathmandu, Pokhara, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur, and Chitwan to bring clarity and elegance to live gatherings—from high-energy lakeside festivals to tech summits and artisan heritage exhibitions.

Curated Excellence

Every listed gathering across Nepal is selected for thought leadership, authentic atmosphere, and distinct cultural value.

Nationwide Discovery

Intuitive navigation makes discovering your next milestone in Kathmandu Valley, Pokhara, Chitwan, and beyond effortless.

Connected Community

Bridging organizers, youth innovators, and attendees to cultivate lifelong networks and celebrate Nepal’s vibrant creative spirit.
